## Global launch date

AION 2 launches globally on **October 5, 2026** on PC via Steam and the Purple launcher. It is a free-to-play MMORPG, with optional Founder's Packs for players who want early access and cosmetics.

## Advance access

Pre-purchasing a Founder's Pack unlocks **Advance Access starting September 30, 2026** — five days before the full release.

## Founder's Packs

- **Standard Founder's Pack** — $24.99, includes the game and the Founder's Pack.
- **Deluxe Founder's Pack** — $49.99, includes the game and the Founder's Pack.
- **Ultimate Founder's Pack** — $99.99, includes the game and the Founder's Pack.

All three editions grant the same five-day advance access window; the higher tiers add more cosmetics and in-game bonuses.

## Platforms & languages

AION 2 is **PC only**, built on **Unreal Engine 5**. The Steam build supports multiple interface and subtitle languages, including English, French, German, Spanish and Japanese, with more announced across regions.

## What to expect at launch

- A world **36 times larger** than the original AION.
- **8 classes** at global launch (with a ninth, the Brawler, live in Korea and Taiwan).
- **Over 200 dungeons** across solo, 5-player and 10-player formats.
- Full 3D aerial combat as a **Daeva**, plus a faction war between Elyos and Asmodians.
