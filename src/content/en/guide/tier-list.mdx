## How to read this tier list

This tier list ranks AION 2's classes across **solo PvP, group PvP, dungeons and farming**. Tiers reflect the current meta on Korean and Taiwan servers ahead of global launch.

## S tier

- **[Gladiator](/classes/gladiator)** — the safest and most complete class. A frontline bruiser with sweeping AoE, lifesteal, heavy crowd control and enough durability to stay in fights that force other damage dealers out.
- **[Assassin](/classes/assassin)** — the sharpest single-target burst. Alignment of buffs produces an enormous number of hits; a skilled Assassin deletes a priority target and disengages.

## A tier

- **[Ranger](/classes/ranger)** — the most complete ranged damage dealer, with consistent damage, mobility, traps and knockbacks. Exceptional for open-world farming.
- **[Cleric](/classes/cleric)** — no other class replaces everything a Cleric brings to a group: healing, cleanses, barriers, defensive cooldowns and resurrection.

## B tier

- **[Sorcerer](/classes/sorcerer)** — a glass cannon with strong burst and control, but positioning controls everything and its survivability is limited.
- **[Spiritmaster](/classes/spiritmaster)** — sustained pet damage and CC resistance, though it relies on skilled pet management to shine.

## Solo PvP quick picks

- **S+ tier:** Assassin, Gladiator, [Templar](/classes/templar) — burst, sustain and near-unbeatable defense.
- **S tier:** the remaining ranged DPS classes for their safe, consistent damage.
- **A tier:** [Chanter](/classes/chanter); **B tier:** Cleric, whose strengths are built for group play.

## Group PvP quick picks

- **S+ tier:** Templar, Cleric — defense, healing and party utility dominate.
- **S tier:** Gladiator, Assassin, Ranger, Sorcerer, Spiritmaster, Chanter.

## The bottom line

Gladiator and Assassin currently define the top of the meta, but every class is viable with the right gear. Use this list as a starting point, not a rule — balance in AION 2 is remarkably healthy.
