## Role overview

The **Chanter** is a hybrid support that blends melee, healing and team buffs into one versatile kit. Armed with a **Staff**, it flexes between damage and support as the fight demands.

## Why play Chanter

- **Team buffs** — attack and utility buffs raise the whole group's performance.
- **Hybrid healing** — heals alongside a [Cleric](/classes/cleric) when needed.
- **Off-damage** — contributes melee pressure while supporting.
- **Flexible** — useful in almost any group composition.

## How it plays

Keep your buffs and heal-over-time effects active, then contribute melee damage between support cycles. A Chanter reads the fight and shifts toward healing or damage depending on what the group needs most.

## Gearing & build tips

- Balance **attack power and healing** to stay flexible.
- Maintain buff uptime — your party's damage depends on it.
- Its healing is weaker than a Cleric's, so complement rather than replace.

## PvE vs PvP

In PvE, Chanter speeds up clears with buffs and covers healing gaps. In PvP, its buffs, cleanses and off-damage make it a strong secondary support in five- and ten-player fights.
