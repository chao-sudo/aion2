## Role overview

The **Assassin** is a stealthy melee DPS that deletes priority targets with rapid back attacks. Armed with a **Dagger**, it thrives on burst windows and repositioning.

## Why play Assassin

- **Explosive burst** — one of the highest single-target bursts in the game.
- **Mobility & stealth** — approach from an advantage and disengage on your terms.
- **Crowd control** — chains that lock down enemies.
- **Top solo PvP** — a skilled Assassin is lethal in 1v1.

## How it plays

Align your buffs, unload your combo in a short window, then reset before the enemy can respond. Back attacks are critical — positioning behind the target multiplies your damage. Longer fights expose your downtime, so pick your moments.

## Gearing & build tips

- Prioritise **crit and attack speed** to compress your burst window.
- Keep an escape plan — mobility is your survivability.
- Squishier than a Gladiator, so learn which mechanics you can afford to trade into.

## PvE vs PvP

In PvE, Assassin is exceptional against bosses and priority targets, especially in shorter fights. In PvP, its burst, invisibility and mobility let it eliminate a target and vanish before the response arrives.
