## Role overview

The **Gladiator** is a frontline bruiser that blends tankiness with serious damage. Using a **Greatsword**, it sweeps through groups with wide AoE strikes and sustains itself through lifesteal.

## Why play Gladiator

- **The safest all-rounder** — durable enough to survive, damaging enough to carry.
- **Sweeping AoE** — cleaves through packs and mob farms quickly.
- **Lifesteal sustain** — keeps health up while attacking.
- **Party buffs** — a perfect block can trigger team-wide damage boosts.

## How it plays

Keep your pressure up at all times: weave AoE attacks between single-target combos and use the Greatsword's lifesteal to stay healthy inside fights that force other damage dealers out. The Gladiator can even off-tank normal dungeons and endgame raids.

## Gearing & build tips

- Stack **attack power and HP** in roughly equal measure.
- Use lifesteal traits to turn sustained combat into survivability.
- The Gladiator competes with the strongest dedicated DPS classes on most leaderboards.

## PvE vs PvP

In PvE, Gladiator is a top-tier solo clearer and a strong group damage dealer. In PvP, its crowd control, durability and sustained pressure make it one of the most complete classes in the game.
