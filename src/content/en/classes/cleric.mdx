## Role overview

The **Cleric** is the main healer of AION 2. Armed with a **Mace**, it keeps groups alive with direct healing, healing over time, cleanses, barriers and resurrection.

## Why play Cleric

- **Unmatched healing** — no other class replaces everything a Cleric brings.
- **Cleanses & barriers** — removes debuffs and shields the party.
- **Resurrection** — rescues teammates who would otherwise stay down.
- **Essential in groups** — every serious dungeon and raid wants a Cleric.

## How it plays

Read the incoming damage and choose the right answer rather than blindly spamming heals. A great Cleric anticipates spikes, cleanses debuffs on time and uses defensive cooldowns to rescue teammates in PvP and PvE alike.

## Gearing & build tips

- Prioritise **healing power and mana regeneration**.
- Keep your defensive cooldowns for burst windows.
- Pair with a [Templar](/classes/templar) to anchor hard-mode groups.

## PvE vs PvP

In PvE, Cleric protection is essential while a group learns fights or takes constant damage. In PvP, its toolkit can reverse a fight, but its low personal damage limits solo play.
