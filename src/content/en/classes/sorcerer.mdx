## Role overview

The **Sorcerer** is AION 2's glass-cannon caster. Using a **Spellbook**, it delivers enormous burst damage and crowd control at range — at the cost of survivability.

## Why play Sorcerer

- **Huge burst** — melts grouped enemies and priority targets.
- **Strong crowd control** — slows, polymorphs and pulses control the battlefield.
- **Smooth skill chains** — cooldown reductions and resets flow one spell into the next.
- **Recently buffed** — one of the top DPS classes on Korean and Taiwan servers.

## How it plays

Positioning controls everything. Stay at range, layer your control abilities and unload your full rotation when you have space. If an enemy closes the gap or interrupts your setup, your limited survivability becomes a serious weakness.

## Gearing & build tips

- Stack **magic attack and cooldown reduction**.
- Save escape and control cooldowns for when melee pressure arrives.
- The **Hibernate stigma** can freeze you in place to recover HP in a pinch.

## PvE vs PvP

In PvE, Sorcerer is the fastest boss-killer in groups but fragile while soloing. In PvP, it punishes groups and priority targets yet demands excellent positioning to stay alive.
