## Role overview

The **Spiritmaster** is a summoner who commands elemental spirits for sustained damage. Armed with an **Orb**, it keeps pressure up through its pets while resisting crowd control.

## Why play Spiritmaster

- **Sustained pet damage** — spirits keep attacking while you reposition.
- **CC resistance** — strong tools against crowd control.
- **Safe pressure** — you can damage from range while your pet holds attention.
- **Unique playstyle** — rewards players who enjoy managing summons.

## How it plays

Summon your spirits, keep your damage-over-time effects rolling and let your pets maintain pressure. Skilled Spiritmasters use their pet's positioning and their own CC resistance to control fights in both PvE and PvP.

## Gearing & build tips

- Balance **magic attack** with survivability — you fight at mid-range.
- Keep your pets summoned and re-summon them the moment they fall.
- Learn which spirits to use for single-target versus group encounters.

## PvE vs PvP

In PvE, Spiritmaster delivers steady damage with strong solo utility. In PvP, its pet pressure and CC resistance make it a frustrating opponent that skilled players exploit to great effect.
